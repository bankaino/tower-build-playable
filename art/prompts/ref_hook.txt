Use case: concept key frame of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, the first screen the viewer sees. A casual tower-stacking game screen, inside the phone screen only (no phone frame).
Subject: a bright clean morning sky (pale blue gradient) with a few soft white clouds and a distant light city skyline in two layers of flat blue-grey silhouettes. From the very top of the frame a construction crane cable hangs down in the centre; a graphite and orange crane hook with two thin ropes holds a single modern apartment floor block (a white and light-grey panel facade with two large windows, an orange-framed loggia, thin cornice on top), swinging slightly above the middle of the frame. Below it, in the lower third, a ready ground-floor lobby block of the same building (wider, glass entrance door with a small canopy, a few steps, a blank orange rounded sign plaque above the door) stands on a small yard: green lawn, light asphalt path, four rounded trees, a bench and a lamp post. A soft orange arrow pointing down with a blank orange rounded plaque above it marks the place to drop the block, centred above the lobby.
HUD (all flat, blank, no text): a large empty orange rounded square at the top left (floor counter place), a white pill at the top right (area counter place) with three small orange house-shaped hearts under it.
Composition: portrait 2:3, symmetric, the lobby and the hanging floor on one vertical axis, lots of calm empty sky, clean and airy.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no phone frame, no watermark, no perspective. Original designs.
